Ten Central Indiana operations, run forward one simulated day at a time. Every operation carries an IB-eligible crop line; five also run a livestock, dairy, apiary, egg, or aquaculture enterprise that's external to IB but shares the same labor, land, and cash. Animal and system care are hard constraints — crop work yields to them, not the other way around.
